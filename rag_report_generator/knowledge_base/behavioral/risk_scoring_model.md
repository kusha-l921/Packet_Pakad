# Phase 4 Deterministic Risk Scoring Model

## Deterministic Indicator-Based Risk Scoring

The Person 2 Phase 4 architecture implements a deterministic, cumulative risk model. Risk scores are not generated by black-box heuristic guesses or generative language models. Instead, the final numerical risk score is computed as the direct sum of points assigned to the observable behavioral indicators triggered by the flow.

The total score is capped on a scale from 0 to 100 points:
$$\text{Risk Score} = \min\left(100, \sum_{i \in \text{Triggered Indicators}} \text{Points}(i)\right)$$

## Risk Severity Tiers

The continuous 0–100 score maps directly to four explicit risk severity tiers:

### LOW (0–24 points)
* **Score Range**: 0 to 24 points.
* **Characterization**: Typical baseline network traffic exhibiting zero or at most one low-impact behavioral anomaly (such as minor directional asymmetry or isolated low-volume periodicity).
* **Operational Meaning**: Flow conforms to expected traffic profiles; standard monitoring suffices.

### MEDIUM (25–49 points)
* **Score Range**: 25 to 49 points.
* **Characterization**: Moderate behavioral anomalies present, such as an unusual packet rate combined with directional asymmetry, or an isolated high-volume upload.
* **Operational Meaning**: Flow warrants informational logging or routine audit; transmission characteristics diverge from standard uniform patterns.

### HIGH (50–74 points)
* **Score Range**: 50 to 74 points.
* **Characterization**: Multiple concurring behavioral indicators detected simultaneously (e.g., unusual high packet rate + unusual high upload volume + burst activity).
* **Operational Meaning**: Significant operational anomaly; flow exhibits multi-dimensional transmission divergence from standard baseline.

### CRITICAL (75–100 points)
* **Score Range**: 75 to 100 points.
* **Characterization**: Extensive compounding anomalies across rate, volume, duration, and directional symmetry (e.g., four or more indicators triggered concurrently).
* **Operational Meaning**: Severe structural anomaly warranting immediate review of communicating endpoints and application context.

## Crucial Principle: Strict Separation of Model Uncertainty and Risk

A fundamental requirement of the Person 2 architecture is the strict mathematical and conceptual decoupling between **Machine Learning Model Uncertainty** and **Behavioral Risk**:

$$\text{Model Uncertainty} \neq \text{Behavioral Risk}$$

1. **Model Uncertainty Contributes Exactly 0 Risk Points**:
   The classification confidence, entropy, or uncertainty produced by the Phase 3 ML classifier contributes precisely **0 points** to the behavioral risk calculation.
2. **Confidence Does Not Equal Risk**:
   * A flow classified as `web` with 99% probability (HIGH confidence) can exhibit CRITICAL behavioral risk if it pushes massive unthrottled outbound volumes at extreme packet rates.
   * A flow classified with LOW confidence (high model uncertainty due to out-of-distribution features) may be completely benign baseline traffic with 0 risk indicators, resulting in a LOW risk score.
3. **Risk Level Is Not Equated with Malicious Intent**:
   Risk scoring quantifies deviation in observable physical transmission patterns. A HIGH or CRITICAL score indicates substantial behavioral anomaly; it does not constitute mathematical or legal proof of malice, attack payload, or compromised host status.
